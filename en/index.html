Our business lines

Import & Export

Trade runs both ways for us: we take what is made in Turkey out to the world, and bring what is made abroad into Turkey. In both directions we run the process from the first enquiry to delivery.

01

Export

We ship the products of the producers we work with to 30 countries across three continents. The buyer may be a retail chain, a wholesaler or an importer — the process is the same.

  • Samples and FOB / CIF quotations
  • Certificates of origin, health and analysis
  • Container loading plan and transport
  • Customs formalities and shipment tracking
Details

02

Import

We import products that are not made in Turkey or that are more competitive abroad. We manage the process from finding the supplier through to customs clearance.

  • Producer research abroad
  • Samples and factory assessment
  • Import permits and regulatory compliance
  • Customs declaration and delivery
Details

How we work

Four steps from enquiry to shipment.

Every job follows the same order, so you always know which stage you are at.

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    We take the product, quantity, packaging and target price.

  2. 02

    Sourcing and samples

    We find the right producer and send samples with a quotation.

  3. 03

    Production and control

    We prepare packaging, labels and documents, and follow production.

  4. 04

    Shipment

    We arrange customs and transport, and complete the delivery.
