What we do

Three lines of business.

Sourcing, export and import. All three come down to the same job: finding the right product at the right price and delivering it on the agreed date.

01

Supply to retail chains

We supply ŞOK, A101, METRO and BİM in food and non-food categories. We find the product at source and bring it up to the chain's standard — packaging and labelling included.

Details

02

Export

We ship the products of the producers we work with to buyers in Europe, Asia and Latin America. To date we have shipped to 30 countries.

Details

03

Import

We bring in products that are not made in Turkey or that are more competitive abroad. We handle supplier selection, customs and logistics.

How we work

Four steps from enquiry to shipment.

Every job follows the same order, so you always know which stage you are at.

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    We take the product, quantity, packaging and target price.

  2. 02

    Sourcing and samples

    We find the right producer and send samples with a quotation.

  3. 03

    Production and control

    We prepare packaging, labels and documents, and follow production.

  4. 04

    Shipment

    We arrange customs and transport, and complete the delivery.
