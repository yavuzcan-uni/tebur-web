Sourcing, export, import.

Three lines of business, one job in all of them: find the right product at the right price, bring it up to standard and deliver it on time.

01 — Sourcing

Supplying retail chains.

Large retail chains look for suppliers both for permanent shelf space and for seasonal promotions. We find the product at home or abroad, bring it up to the chain's packaging, labelling and documentation standard, and plan the shipment.

Product research and pricing

We search for the requested product among producers in Turkey and abroad, and present it with costs and delivery terms.

Packaging and labelling

Under the chain's own brand or the producer's. Local-language labels, barcodes and regulatory information included.

Shipment planning

We set the production and delivery schedule against the promotion date and follow it through to warehouse delivery.

02 — Export

Shipping to 30 countries.

We deliver the products of the producers we work with to buyers abroad. Whether the buyer is a retail chain, a wholesaler or an importer, the process is the same: sample, price, documents, shipment.

Samples and quotation

We send samples of the requested product and quote on an FOB or CIF basis.

Documentation

We prepare the certificates of origin, health certificates and analysis reports required for export, together with the producer.

Containers and logistics

We organise the loading plan, transport and customs formalities.

03 — Import

From abroad into the Turkish market.

We import products that are not made in Turkey or that are more competitive elsewhere. We run the process from finding the supplier through to customs clearance.

Supplier search and assessment

We research producers abroad and assess them on samples and factory information.

Customs and regulation

We handle import permits, customs declarations and product compliance.

Delivery

We route incoming goods according to the buyer's delivery schedule.

Process

Four steps from enquiry to shipment.

Every job follows the same order, so you always know which stage you are at.

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    We take the product, quantity, packaging and target price.

  2. 02

    Sourcing and samples

    We find the right producer and send samples with a quotation.

  3. 03

    Production and control

    We prepare packaging, labels and documents, and follow production.

  4. 04

    Shipment

    We arrange customs and transport, and complete the delivery.

Which product are you looking for?

Tell us the product, the quantity and the target market, and we will start the search straight away.
