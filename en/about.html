Sourcing and foreign trade, from Istanbul.

TEBUR Dış Ticaret Ltd. Şti. was founded in 2011. We supply Turkey's large retail chains and use the same producer network to export to three continents.

The company

We started in 2011 with one job.

The company was set up to supply Turkey's leading retail chains with both imported and domestic products, across a range of categories.

Since then we have worked with ŞOK, A101, METRO and BİM across a range of categories. Those relationships gave us two things: experience in managing high-volume orders, and first-hand knowledge of what the chains require in quality terms.

We began exporting with the same producer network. Today we ship to buyers in Europe, Asia and Latin America.

Our aim is straightforward: to offer products made in Turkey at high quality standards and competitive prices, to global customers and to the local market.

How we work

Trade built on trust, repeated over time.

Fair, ethical and regular trade is not a slogan for us — it is how the business runs. Four points sum it up.

  • Partnership based on trust

    We work for the long term. We stand behind the price and the delivery date we give; if something changes, you hear it from us first.

  • Fair and ethical trade

    We work transparently between producer and buyer. Cost components, packaging and delivery terms are open to both sides.

  • Regular shipments

    We would rather build a repeating shipment programme than close one-off deals. Predictable volume means a better price for everyone.

  • Retail chain standard

    We prepare products to a standard that passes the supplier audits of large retail chains, and apply the same rigour to export orders.
