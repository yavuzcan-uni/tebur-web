Food and non-food product groups.

The items below are the ones we have worked with most. Our portfolio is not a fixed catalogue — if what you are looking for is not listed, we will run a sourcing search for it.

Group 01

Packaged food

Long shelf-life bakery products and snacks, in retail and bulk packaging, prepared to the shelf standards of retail chains.

Biscuits and cookies

Oat, chocolate chip and plain varieties. Single and family packs.

Hazelnut chocolate biscuits

Filled and coated varieties, for promotions and permanent shelf space.

Crispy pastry

Chocolate, lemon and vanilla filled varieties.

Puff pastry products

Apricot, cherry and strawberry filled puff pastries.

Croissants

Cocoa, strawberry, apricot and vanilla filling options.

Grissini and breadsticks

Plain and flavoured varieties, in single packs or boxes.

Group 02

Frozen food

Cold-chain products, ready to bake in store or at the plant. Shipping and storage temperatures are confirmed at the quotation stage.

Raw puff pastry dough

Sheet and portioned formats, in industrial and retail packaging.

Strudel

Apple, cherry and strawberry filled varieties.

Sweet buns and rolls

Blueberry, chocolate and cinnamon varieties.

Ready-to-bake croissants

Filled and plain, portioned for in-store ovens.

Group 03

Non-food household products

Products we supply for the seasonal home and kitchen aisles of retail chains. In this group we usually work to a specific promotion date.

Ceramic tableware

Bowls, plates and serving pieces in a range of colours and forms.

Glass sweet and sugar bowls

Lidded glass serveware for gifting and holiday seasons.

Footed cake stands

Glass stand and base, with cover.

Glass storage containers

Kitchen storage and serving range in various capacities.

Kitchen utensils

Small kitchen helpers and serving accessories.

Home care products

Lint rollers and similar practical cleaning items.
