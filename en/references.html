Why it matters

An audited supplier.

We have worked with ŞOK, A101, METRO and BİM across different categories. All of these chains audit their suppliers on documentation, production conditions and delivery performance.

For a buyer abroad, that means one thing: we have the practice of preparing a product to retail chain standard and keeping a high-volume order on schedule.

Case

A101 — seasonal promotion supply

We supplied products for the seasonal promotions of A101 Yeni Mağazacılık A.Ş. In work like this the hard part is not the product — it is the calendar.

Scope of work

Finding the product at source, preparing packaging and barcodes to the chain's standard, completing labels and documentation, and following the shipment through to warehouse delivery.

The calendar

The promotion date is fixed and the product has to be on the shelf that day. Production and shipping were scheduled backwards from that date, with no room for delay.
